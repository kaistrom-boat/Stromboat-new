▸ ABOUT · THE LAUNCH DOCK

Small boats. Endless storms.

What Stromboat is, where the boats come from, and what we promise — which is deliberately little.

The idea

Every harbour has one dock where the small boats leave from — too small for the cargo routes, too stubborn to stay tied up. Stromboat is that dock, drawn in pixels: 1,333 small boats, each leaving exactly once, each into weather it did not choose.

A boat's storm is decided by its number before it ever touches the water. Calm grey for most. Teal rain for some. And for twenty-six of them, the eye of the storm itself — dead calm, a giant lantern, and a halo where the sky should be.

The fleet

  • 4 hulls — Dinghy, Junk, Longship, Lantern Raft
  • 5 seas — Calm Grey, Squall Teal, Night Violet, Thunder Gold, Eye of the Storm
  • 20 artworks — every hull painted in every sea, in one night-dock style: ink-dark water, a single amber light
  • 1,333 tokens — 667 Common, 333 Rare, 200 Epic, 107 Legendary, 26 Mythic, fixed before launch and published in the metadata

How it launches

Two lanes, both open from the first moment. Holders of a Binance Agent ID claim one free Common — the dock's way of saying the agent fleet is welcome here. Everyone else, agents included, buys from the paid lane at 0.00144 BNB, where the good weather lives: Rare and up, never Common. The full rules are in the Docs.

What we promise

An artwork. No token, no roadmap, no promised returns. The boats are the product. If that is enough — welcome aboard. If it is not, the dock wishes you fair winds elsewhere, honestly.

Stromboat sails on BNB Chain. Announcements come only from @stromboatcom and this site. The contract addresses live on the Contracts page.
