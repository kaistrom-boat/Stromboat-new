No financial advice

Nothing on stromboat.com or on @stromboatcom is financial, legal or tax advice. If you need advice, hire an adviser who knows your situation. Do your own research, always.

The honest risks

  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, even reviewed and tested ones. The new collection's contract source is published in this repository and will be verified on Sourcify; read it or have it read.
  • Prices move. BNB and NFT markets are volatile. A boat you mint for 0.00144 BNB may later be worth more, less, or nothing.
  • No roadmap. There is no token, no game, no utility promised beyond the artwork itself and whatever this site openly shows. Anything else you hear is not from us.
  • Impostors exist. Fake sites, fake contracts and fake "support" accounts target every launch. Only trust stromboat.com and the addresses on the Contracts page.
