3 · Mint rules

  • Minting is governed entirely by the smart contract published on the Contracts page. If anything on this site disagrees with the contract, the contract wins.
  • Free lane: one Common per wallet, one claim per Binance Agent ID, while the free pool (IDs 1–667) lasts.
  • Paid lane: 0.00144 BNB per boat (or the price the contract currently reports), maximum 7 per wallet, while the paid pool (IDs 668–1,333) lasts.
  • All mint transactions are final and irreversible once confirmed on-chain. Network (gas) fees are paid to the network, not to us, and are never refundable.
