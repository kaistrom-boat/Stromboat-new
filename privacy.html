▸ LEGAL · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy policy.

Short, because there is little to tell: this site has no accounts and sells no data. Last updated: 17 November 2026 (UTC).

What we collect

Nothing directly. stromboat.com has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletters and no comment sections. We do not ask for your name, email or documents, and we never see your seed phrase or private keys — your wallet talks to the blockchain, not to us.

What the infrastructure sees

  • Hosting. This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address and browser details when you visit, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Blockchain data. When you mint, your wallet address and the transaction become part of the public BNB Chain ledger — visible to everyone, forever, by design. That includes the wallet checker on this site, which reads public contract data.
  • RPC providers. The live counters on the home page query public BNB Chain RPC endpoints; those providers see the queries, not your identity.

Cookies

The site itself sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The deploy console page stores one thing locally in your browser — the contract address you deployed or loaded — so it can restore it on your next visit. It never leaves your device.

Your choices

Use a fresh wallet for minting if you prefer to keep it separate from your other activity. For anything privacy-related, reach us on X · @stromboatcom.
