▸ HARBOUR LOG · NOTES

A few honest answers.

How many are there?

1,333. Four hulls across five seas — twenty artworks, and every token’s hull and sea are fixed by its number.

What decides my boat?

Its token number. Numbers 1–667 are the free Common pool; 668–1,333 are the paid rarities. Every number was assigned its hull and sea before launch — the same number always draws the same boat.

How much does it cost?

Two lanes. FREE: one Common boat, 0 BNB, with a Binance Agent ID — max 1 per wallet. PAID: 0.00144 BNB per boat, Rare and up only, max 7 per wallet. This page reads the price live from the contract; send exactly price × quantity, nothing more.

Where does the art live?

Each boat is one of twenty night-dock artworks — its hull in its sea — served from this site.

What comes with it?

An artwork. No token promises, no roadmap.
